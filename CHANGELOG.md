# Watchspire Changelog

### Version: 1.0.0 ###
* **New:** WordPress site-health dashboard with a unified operational overview, 7/30/90-day look-backs and a custom start/end date range
* **New:** Form delivery monitoring for Contact Form 7, WPForms, Gravity Forms, Elementor Pro Forms, Fluent Forms, and Forminator
* **New:** Submission Gap Detection that learns normal form activity before alerting on unusual silence
* **New:** Mail Health & Risk Score with WordPress mail-failure tracking and configuration diagnostics
* **New:** SSL certificate expiry monitoring, read directly from your own server over TLS, with hostname validation and advance expiry warnings
* **New:** 404 and 5xx server-error monitoring with aggregated counts to avoid unnecessary database growth
* **New:** Uptime self-checks for the homepage and one configurable key page
* **New:** Soft-404, maintenance-mode, and broken-page detection
* **New:** Broken Links & Images scanner with opt-in background scanning, pause, resume, cancel, recheck, and ignore controls
* **New:** WordPress Change Log for plugin, theme, core, auto-update, activation, deactivation, deletion, and selected settings events
* **New:** AI crawler activity tracking for GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, and other supported crawlers, with a per-bot breakdown
* **New:** robots.txt visibility for supported AI crawlers
* **New:** Alert deduplication, rate limiting, and recovery notifications
* **New:** Weekly Health Summary with important monitoring activity
* **New:** Configurable data-retention, export, and privacy controls
* **New:** Background scheduling through Action Scheduler with safeguards for constrained hosting environments
* **New:** Dismissible welcome and review notices, shown only outside WatchSpire's own screens
* **New:** Dashboard, Settings, Support and rating links on the Plugins screen
* **New:** Extension hooks so companion add-ons can extend the dashboard, monitors, alert channels, integrations, and settings
* No third-party services. WatchSpire's only outbound requests are to your own site, and — once you enable link scanning — to the URLs your own content already links to
* Initial release
